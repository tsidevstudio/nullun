# Nullun Troubleshooting

## A design does not look as expected

- Recheck battery, carrier, signal and Wi-Fi settings
- Confirm the selected style
- Review background settings
- Reopen the preview before external output

## History does not show what I expect

Confirm that you are viewing the correct previous item and that the app was not cleared or removed between sessions.

## External output is unavailable

Confirm whether the Lifetime unlock is active.
